🔒 Privacy Policy — NoteWav AI

Last updated: 2026

NoteWav AI ("we", "the app") converts study notes into audio narration and mind maps for students in Sri Lanka. This page explains what data we collect and how it's used.

1. What we collect

  • Note content: Text you type, or text extracted from images you upload (via OCR), is sent to Google's Gemini AI and Google Cloud Vision to generate audio scripts and mind maps.
  • Account info (only if you sign in with Google): Your name, email, and profile picture, used to sync your Library, coins, and study streak across devices.
  • Usage data: An anonymous device ID, approximate device/browser type, and which features you use (e.g. "note processed", "audio generated") — used only to understand overall app usage, not to identify you personally.
  • Local device storage: Preferences like app language, font size, and playback speed are stored only in your browser (localStorage) and never sent to our servers.

2. How we use it

  • To generate your podcast script, audio, and mind map (via Google Gemini, Google Cloud Vision, and Google Text-to-Speech).
  • To let you save and revisit notes in your Library.
  • To keep the app running reliably (error monitoring, rate-limit protection against abuse).
  • To understand which features are actually used, so we can improve the app.

3. Third-party services we use

Your note content is processed by: Google Gemini (script/mind-map generation), Google Cloud Vision (OCR), and Google Text-to-Speech (gTTS). If you sign in, we use Google Sign-In for authentication. Your data (notes, account info) is stored on Turso, a hosted database provider. We may also use Sentry for error monitoring — this only receives technical error details, never your note content.

4. Children's privacy

NoteWav AI is built for students, some of whom may be under 18. We do not knowingly collect more personal information than necessary (just an optional Google account for sync), and we do not sell any data to third parties. Parents/guardians with concerns can contact us using the details below.

5. Your choices

  • You can use NoteWav AI as a guest without ever signing in — no account data is collected.
  • If you signed in, you can sign out or delete your saved notes at any time from the Library.
  • To request full account deletion, contact us (below).

6. Advertising

If NoteWav AI shows optional rewarded ads in the future (e.g. "watch an ad for coins"), those ads are served by Google's ad network and may use their own cookies/identifiers per Google's own advertising policies. This is entirely optional and never required to use the core app.
